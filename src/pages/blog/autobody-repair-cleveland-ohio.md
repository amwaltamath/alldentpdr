## When you need autobody repair vs. PDR

- **PDR** is ideal when the metal can be reshaped and the **factory paint is intact** (many hail dents, door dings, and light impacts).
- **Autobody repair** is the right path when paint is broken, panels need replacement, or metal is too stretched for paintless methods.

A shop that does both can give you an honest panel-by-panel plan instead of repainting everything by default. Our comparison guide — [PDR vs. traditional body shop](/blog/pdr-vs-body-shop) — walks through cost, time, and resale value.

## What autobody work typically includes

Common collision and body shop jobs in the Cleveland area include:

- Bumper cover repair or replacement  
- Door, fender, and quarter panel repair  
- Scratch and paint refinish  
- Blend work to match existing color  
- Insurance-supported collision restoration  

## Insurance and choosing your shop

Ohio drivers can **choose their own repair facility** for covered claims. Your carrier may suggest a preferred shop, but you are not required to use it. Document damage with photos, file your claim, and bring (or submit) an inspection request so the repair scope is clear before work starts.
