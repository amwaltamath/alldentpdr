## What to do within 24 hours of a Bedford hail storm

Acting quickly after a hail storm protects both your vehicle and your insurance claim.

### 1. Photograph everything before moving the car

Document the damage in good outdoor lighting — hood, roof, trunk lid, upper door panels, rear quarter panels, and the windshield/back glass. Take wide shots and close-ups. Time-stamp the photos so they clearly tie to the storm event.

### 2. File your insurance claim right away

Call your insurance carrier or file through their app while the storm is recent. Hail damage falls under **comprehensive coverage** — it does not affect your collision rates. Your deductible still applies, but for moderate-to-severe storms, most repairs are fully covered above the deductible.

### 3. Choose your repair shop before the adjuster does

Ohio drivers have the legal right to choose their own repair shop. Your insurer may suggest a preferred shop — you are not required to use it. If you want factory-quality paintless dent repair with your paint fully preserved, choose a specialist like All Dent PDR rather than a general body shop.

## Common questions from Bedford drivers after a hail storm

**Can I still drive my car after hail damage?**
Yes — hail dents are cosmetic. Unless a window cracked or the roof is structurally compromised, the vehicle is safe to drive. That said, don't delay — Ohio storms can cause rust to form in chips within weeks.

**How long do I have to file a hail damage claim in Ohio?**
Most Ohio auto policies require claims to be filed within 30–60 days of the damage event. Check your policy. The longer you wait, the harder it is to prove the damage came from a specific storm.

**Will getting hail damage repaired increase my insurance rates?**
Comprehensive claims (including hail) generally do not increase your premiums the way at-fault collision claims do. Policies vary — check with your carrier — but most Ohio drivers see no rate increase from a single hail claim.
