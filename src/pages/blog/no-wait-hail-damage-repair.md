## Why body shops create long waits after storms

When hail hits a region, every shop gets flooded at once. Traditional body shops often:

- Route hail jobs through **paint and filler** workflows that take longer
- Stack storm work behind existing collision repairs
- Run short on painters, estimators, and rental-car coordination staff
- Book out **weeks in advance** during peak season

Paintless dent repair (PDR) is different. When paint is intact, technicians work dents from behind the panel — **no sanding, no bondo, no repaint booth**. That is why PDR shops like ours can move hail jobs faster while preserving your factory finish.
