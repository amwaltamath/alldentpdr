## Why paintless dent repair is usually the right method

Hail damage often leaves shallow dents while the paint stays unbroken. In those cases, **paintless dent repair (PDR)** is typically the preferred solution because it:

- Preserves your original factory paint
- Avoids fillers and repainting
- Protects resale value better than conventional refinishing
- Reduces downtime compared with many body-shop repairs
- Produces a cleaner, more factory-correct result when performed properly

For Bedford-area hail damage, that means your car can often be restored without the extra materials and finish changes associated with traditional body repair.
