## What affects your PDR price?

### 1. Dent size

Larger dents require more time and skill to reshape. A dime-sized parking lot ding takes a fraction of the work compared to a softball-sized dent on your hood.

**Rule of thumb:** the bigger the dent, the higher the price — but PDR is almost always still cheaper than body shop repair for the same damage.

### 2. Dent location on the vehicle

Not all panels are equal. Some are easy to access from behind; others require removing interior trim, headliners, or taillight assemblies.

**Easier (lower cost):**
- Flat door panels
- Hood and trunk lid (most cases)
- Fenders with open wheel well access

**More complex (higher cost):**
- Roof rails and A-pillars
- Double-panel areas (door edges with bracing)
- Panels near structural reinforcements
- Aluminum body panels (common on newer vehicles)

### 3. Number of dents

Hail damage is priced **per panel**, not per individual dent. A hood with 40 hail dents costs the same as a hood with 80 hail dents — you're paying for panel access and restoration, not counting each ding.

For scattered door dings across multiple panels, each affected panel is quoted separately.

### 4. Creases vs. round dents

Round, shallow dents are the sweet spot for PDR. **Creases** — especially sharp ones along body lines — require more precision and time. A crease on a door edge may cost 30–50% more than a round dent of the same size.

### 5. Paint condition

PDR only works when the factory paint is intact. If paint is cracked, chipped, or flaking, conventional body repair (with repainting) is required — and costs significantly more:

| | PDR | Body Shop Repair |
|---|---|---|
| **Small door ding** | $75 – $150 | $400 – $800+ |
| **Single panel hail** | $300 – $800 | $1,000 – $2,500+ |
| **Factory paint** | Preserved | Removed and repainted |
| **Turnaround** | Hours to days | Days to weeks |

## PDR cost by repair type

### Door ding repair cost

Door dings are the most common PDR repair — and the most affordable.

- **Single small ding:** $75 – $125
- **Two to three dings on one door:** $125 – $200
- **Multiple dings across several doors:** $200 – $400

In the Cleveland and Bedford area, most single door ding repairs are completed in under two hours at your home or workplace.

### Hail damage repair cost

Hail is priced per panel. Here's what drivers typically see:

- **Hood only:** $250 – $600
- **Roof only:** $300 – $700
- **Hood + roof:** $500 – $1,200
- **Full vehicle (moderate hail):** $1,500 – $4,000
- **Full vehicle (severe hail):** $3,000 – $6,000+

**Insurance note:** Comprehensive auto insurance usually covers hail damage in full, minus your deductible. PDR hail repair is almost always covered — and filing a hail claim typically does not raise your premiums.

See our full guide: [How to File an Insurance Claim for Hail Damage](/blog/hail-damage-insurance-claim-guide).

### Hood and trunk dent repair cost

Flat horizontal panels are generally straightforward for PDR:

- **Single dent on hood or trunk:** $150 – $350
- **Multiple dents on one panel:** $250 – $500
- **Large or complex hood dent:** $350 – $600

![Hood dent restored with paintless dent repair](/images/pdrhoodafter.png)

### Crease and body line dent repair cost

Creases require more skill and time:

- **Light crease (under 6 inches):** $200 – $400
- **Medium crease along a body line:** $350 – $550
- **Sharp or stretched crease:** $500+ (may not qualify for PDR)

If you're unsure whether your crease qualifies, send us photos — we'll tell you honestly.
